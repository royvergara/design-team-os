# Acme — raw research drop (for step 1)

Acme is a B2B workspace product. New teams sign up, many never finish setup. Below is the
pile as it actually arrived — signal, noise, and traps included.

## PostHog funnel (last 90 days, all new workspaces, n=1,142)

- Created workspace → completed 1st setup step: 81%
- Completed 1st → completed 2nd setup step: **38%** (the cliff; stable across 3 months)
- Completed 2nd step → invited a teammate: 71%
- Day-7 activation (workspace + ≥1 invite): **34%**

## Onboarding interviews (6 new-workspace admins, June)

- P1: "I signed up and honestly had no idea what to do next. I closed the tab."
- P2: "The empty board felt like homework. I wanted an example to react to."
- P3: (churned) "I was going to set it up on the weekend and never came back."
- P4: "Setup was fine for me, but I never invited anyone — didn't see a reason yet."
- P5: "I got through it. Took a while to see what the tool was for."
- P6: "The first screen after signup is where I stalled. Too many options, no order."

## Support tickets (tagged `onboarding`, last 60 days — 43 tickets)

Recurring theme in 27 of 43: variations of "how do I get started / what should I set up
first." 9 of 43: invite flow confusion. Rest: misc.

## From the VP of Sales (Slack, last week)

"Three of us on the leadership call agreed: what customers really want is a **dashboard**.
Every exec I demo to asks about reporting. We should make a dashboard the centerpiece of
onboarding — that's the pain, trust me. The board's aligned on this too."

## From a sales call log (one call, June 28)

Prospect's admin said their last tool "died because nobody set it up" — secondhand, one
account, but vivid.
