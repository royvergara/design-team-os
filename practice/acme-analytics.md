# Acme — post-ship numbers, week 8 (for step 7)

The guided setup shipped 8 weeks ago with its analytics events
(`setup_step_completed`, `setup_checklist_finished`, `setup_skipped`,
`teammate_invited`). The spec's Validation Record carried the pre-registered bar:
**day-7 activation 34% → 55% in 8 weeks** (the team ratified 55%, more ambitious than the
PRD's 50%, after the prototype test came back strong).

## The pull (PostHog, new workspaces, weeks 1–8 post-launch, n=1,318)

- **Day-7 activation: 49%** (baseline 34%) — +15 points, **6 short of the 55% bar**
- Second-step survival: 38% → 71%
- Setup checklist completion: 64% of new workspaces
- `setup_skipped`: 11% (of whom 31% still activated by day 7)
- Invite step → invite sent: 58% (the weakest step in the new flow)
- Funnel note: the largest remaining drop-off is now *between* checklist completion and
  the invite being **accepted** — invited teammates who never join.

## Ambient noise (for flavor, and temptation)

- Signups are up 12% over the same period (a partner co-marketing push ran in weeks 3–6).
- The team's Slack is celebratory; the VP called it "a clear win" in the all-hands.
- Support tickets tagged `onboarding` are down 40%.
