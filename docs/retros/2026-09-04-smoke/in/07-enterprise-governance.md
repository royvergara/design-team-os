What we have: 6 business units, roughly 40 designers using AI tooling (v0, Figma AI, internal LLM). Three units keep work ledgers as of this quarter; the other three do not. Of the ledgered units, last quarter: 34 efforts shipped, 21 have a ledger, 12 of those have a bar ratified before build, 7 have a measured outcome against that bar. Two open bets, one past its review date.

The CRO's actual question is "can you prove AI isn't shipping unreviewed work?" I need to answer that honestly in a scorecard the committee reads. What does the scorecard say, and what do I do about the three units with nothing?
