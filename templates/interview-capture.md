# Interview capture — one file per session

The execution bridge between a validation plan and its read. `validation-plan` designs the
test and pre-registers the decision rules; this template makes _running_ it the path of
least resistance — the step that otherwise quietly never happens (a plan with scripts,
scaffolds, and an empty log is the most common way a validation arc dies).

Copy once per session into wherever the plan says results live (typically
`design-os.reviews/`), named `<work-slug>-interview-NN.md`. Fill it **during or immediately
after** the session — a capture reconstructed a week later is a memory, not a record. Every
tally row below must map onto a decision rule the plan pre-registered; if you find yourself
adding a tally the plan never named, that is a new hypothesis for the _next_ plan, not a
criterion for this one.

plan tracks as surfaced / not surfaced. Do not introduce the product, the concept, or any
loaded vocabulary in this half — the moment you name it, everything after is prompted.

## Surprise register

Anything that fit no hypothesis — the unanticipated use, the critique that keeps returning,
the audience you didn't target who leaned in. Do not force these into the tallies; they are
next-plan input.

The one line worth carrying into the plan's Result section, verbatim.
